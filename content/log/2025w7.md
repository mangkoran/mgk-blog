## Other

- Perforce CLI is suprisingly usable. My Perforce worfklow:
  - Check/set stream in client: `p4 client`
  - Sync files: `p4 sync`
  - Open files: `p4 edit`, or
  - Restore shelved files from CL: `p4 unshelve`
  - Check opened files: `p4 opened`
  - Shelve opened files: `p4 shelve`
  - Create review for the CL in Swarm. You can even replace CL in review
  - <https://help.perforce.com/helix-core/server-apps/cmdref/current/Content/CmdRef/Home-cmdref.html>
